**Hello!** I’m _Sean_. My professional mission is to _make computers better for human thinking and creativity_. I'm a software design and engineering leader who is deeply interested in the intersections between design, philosophy, cognitive science and computing.

I’m originally from California, and live with my wife and kids in the San Francisco bay area. I studied computer science at [UCLA](https://www.ucla.edu) and at the (radically interdisciplinary and sadly now shuttered) Arts Computation Engineering program at [UC Irvine](https://www.uci.edu). 

![A portrait of the author.](/assets/images/sean_voisen_wide.webp
"A portrait of the author.")

Currently, I lead teams of engineers and design technologists building design systems and new user experiences for web, mobile and desktop at Adobe. Before that I built collaboration and accessibility features for [Adobe Express](https://express.adobe.com), managed CSS layout, rendering and accessibility engineering teams working on [Firefox](https://mozilla.org), and made web-based tools for animated comics at [Madefire](https://techcrunch.com/2022/04/29/madefire-shuts-down/).
